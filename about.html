About RR Interiors & Furniture

We blend furniture craftsmanship with interior design thinking to create spaces that feel elevated and livable — for homes in Delhi, Noida and Tirunelveli.

Our Story

RR Interiors & Furniture began as a local workshop with one vision: help people experience better interiors without complexity. Today that workshop operates as a full interior practice running projects across Delhi NCR and Tirunelveli, with a team of designers, craftsmen and installers handling every stage — planning, build, delivery and handover — in-house. More than 500 homes have been refreshed with our furniture and interior work, and every one of them started with the same published rate sheet and a written quotation.

Why customers choose us

  • Interior-led recommendations, not just standalone products.
  • One team across Delhi, Noida and Tirunelveli — single point of contact from measurement to installation.
  • Premium materials with modern finishes and enduring comfort.
  • Design support for layouts, tones, and visual balance.
  • Published per sq. ft. rates, so budgets start honest.
  • Dependable customer guidance from planning to delivery.
